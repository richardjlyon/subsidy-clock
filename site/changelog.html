Change log

Every change to a figure this site publishes is logged here. There are two kinds. A correction means a published figure, or its description, was wrong. A change means the method, the scope or a source was changed on purpose, and a published figure moved as a result.

Corrections are also listed on their own on the corrections page, which is where to report an error. Both pages read the same log, so they cannot disagree.

This log began on 10 October 2026. Earlier changes were recorded on that date from the site’s public repository history, and each such entry says so in its note. Their figures are the ones the site published either side of each change.
